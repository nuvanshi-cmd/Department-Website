About the Department

The Department of Mathematics at Sri Venkateswara College offers an academic environment where students explore mathematical ideas, develop logical thinking and learn various problem-solving techniques.

The department focuses on building a strong foundation in mathematics through classroom teaching, assignments, discussions and academic activities.

Courses Offered

The undergraduate mathematics programme covers various branches of mathematics that help students develop theoretical knowledge and practical understanding.

Algebra

Study of groups, rings, fields and algebraic structures.

Calculus

Understanding derivatives, integrals and their applications.

Real Analysis

Study of sequences, limits, continuity and functions.

Linear Algebra

Vectors, matrices, linear transformations and equations.

Differential Equations

Methods of solving equations involving derivatives.

Discrete Mathematics

Logic, relations, sets, graphs and mathematical structures.
